# Drone Quest – Tromelin Island equipment map

Project for the **Sistemas de Comunicação Multimédia** course (University of Aveiro, 2026/27): a multimedia communication system for a drone race on Tromelin Island.

## Interactive map

Open the GitHub Pages link, or open `index.html` in a browser (it also works offline).

Equipment map of the island: race track with checkpoints CP0–CP7, 8 ground cameras, technical hub, camera links, VSAT satellite link and the 8 drones flying. Tap any point or line to read its details.

Two buttons at the top choose the version, as in the final report:
- **Low-Cost**: 1080p · 30 fps, cameras linked by radio (5 GHz), 2D graphics, drone positions once per second, VSAT 10 Mbps.
- **Premium**: 1080p · 60 fps, cameras linked by optical fibre, AR/3D graphics, drone positions 5 times per second, VSAT 15 Mbps.
